# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

This project welcomes everyone. We want taking part, whether by filing an issue,
asking a question, or sending a pull request, to be a respectful experience for
all, regardless of experience level or background.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate in all interactions.
- Give and accept constructive feedback in good faith.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the people learning from it.
- Assume good intent and ask for clarification before assuming the worst.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Discriminatory or derogatory comments.
- Publishing someone's private information without their consent.
- Sustained disruption of discussion.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, and any
other channel tied to the project, and when an individual is representing the
project in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer through the email on their GitHub
profile. Reports will be reviewed and handled confidentially. The maintainer may
remove, edit, or reject contributions, comments, and other interactions that do
not align with this code, and may take any action they consider appropriate, up
to a temporary or permanent ban from the project.
